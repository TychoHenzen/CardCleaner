# Checkout, Shelves and Prices

Read this when you change the checkout counter, payment, shelves, shelf levels and seats, or prices and price tags.
Moved out of CLAUDE.md, which keeps only the rules most tasks need. Notes about this area belong here.

## Checkout

The shop's checkout counter (`World/Storefront/CheckoutCounter`, a `CheckoutCounter` that is also the production `ICheckoutHandoff`) takes customers' baskets: a `Customer` (`ICheckoutCustomer`) hands over its basket at the counter and waits in one of the four `QueueSlot` markers. The front customer's items appear as scannable props on the `ItemSpot` markers, which sit on the counter's deck (0.62 m), and the player scans each by interacting with it. A scanned item goes into the bag: it shrinks away and stops colliding at once, and the items stacked on it settle into its place. The counter collides as three boxes that follow its art: the 0.64 m deck (`CollisionShape3D`, the shape the checkout tests aim at), the register (`RegisterShape`) and the display case (`DisplayShape`). A customer that implements `IBasketLooks` keeps a still copy of each item's meshes when it hands the basket over (`StockItemLook`: no script, collision or physics, a flat item laid on its widest face), so each prop shows the real card, booster or pack being sold; a customer without it gets plain stand-in props. Then the payment screen (`CheckoutPaymentUi`, which claims the cursor through `ICursorOwner`) takes the card total on a keypad (`CheckoutKeypadPanel`, whose readout shows the typed amount beside the total due), or the cash change by a denomination picker (`CheckoutChangePanel`, whose readout shows the amount due, the cash handed over, the change owed and the change picked so far). A correct entry adds the basket total to `IMoneyService` once, frees the props and tells the customer to leave; Escape closes the screen without paying, and interacting with the counter reopens it. The counter is on the interaction layer only while a scanned sale waits for the player's payment (`CheckoutCounter.CanInteract`), so at any other time the aim passes through it to the scan props and to anything lying on it. Its register display (`RegisterDisplay`, a two-sided screen `SignBoard` on a pole on the register) shows the sale as a till does (`CheckoutCounterText`: `Items 1 of 3` with the scanned subtotal, `Card payment` with the total, `Cash payment` with the total and the cash given, `Cashier serving`) with the last message under it, and nothing while the counter is idle; the balance is on the `ShopHud`, which sits on a dark backing so it reads against the lit ceiling. A customer whose plan is empty (nothing on the sales shelves it could take) walks to a shelf level (`ShelfOfferIndex.BrowsePoints`, taken in turn), looks for `Customer.BrowseSeconds`, complains "nothing in stock" (`RefusalReason.NothingInStock`) and leaves; a customer who leaves empty-handed for any reason complains once. `InstantCheckout` is the instant stand-in that the customer test suites register, and `CheckoutSession` and `PaymentRules` are the Godot-free rules. The card shelf (`Scenes/Shop/Items/Shelf.tscn`, a `CardShelf`) is a two-board display case (`SI_Prop_Shelf_09` of the SIMPLE Shop Interiors pack at `ArtScale` 60) with two levels of four one-card seats (`DeckSlot`s): the `Stands` level, four card stands (`Scenes/Shop/Items/CardStand.tscn`) on its top, each holding a card upright and leaning back 25 degrees, and the `Rests` level, four card rests (`Scenes/Shop/Items/CardRest.tscn`) on its middle board, each holding a card leaning back 78 degrees on an acrylic wedge. The case's opaque top covers its whole depth, so a card standing inside it was hidden from a standing player; the bottom compartment holds no slot for the same reason. A card shows the top of its texture at its local -Z, so each slot is turned with its -Z pointing up and back. Each slot's acrylic parts sit under an `Upright` node that undoes the slot's turn, so the base lies level. `ShelfSeatTest` reads the card's top edge from the card mesh (its smallest UV v), fails a card shown upside down, and fails when a shelf collider or price tag blocks the line from a standing eye (1.8 m high, 1.2 m in front) to a card's centre or the middle of its top edge. A slot seats what it locks at `DeckSlot.SeatPosition`: `PositionOffset`, plus `StackOffset` for each body below it, both in the slot's own frame, and takes the slot's rotation, so stock stays put on a turned shelf. Every shelf collides as boxes that follow its model's parts (boards, sides, posts), not one block, and each slot sits on a board of the model: `ShelfSeatTest` reads the up-facing faces of the loaded art mesh and fails when stock or a stand floats above its board or sinks into it, and `FurnitureCatalogTest` fails when a shelf's `FurnitureItem` size differs from its colliders. A card saved under a slot is locked back into that slot at the seat the slot has now (`SavedCardRespawner`), so a shelf whose seats moved still gets its cards back on load; containers still match a seat by position (`SeatAt`, within 5 cm), so a container saved on a product-shelf or rack seat that has since moved further loads loose at its saved position. A slot claims only free stock: `CanTake` refuses a body another slot holds, and a body is in the slot's list before it is reparented. A reparent reports the body entering every trigger it overlaps again at once, so before this rule two slots whose triggers both reached a container took it from each other without end, until Godot's deferred-call queue overflowed and the process crashed (`DeckSlotContainerTest.ABoosterInsideTwoSlotTriggersIsLockedByOneSlotOnly`). The player stocks and takes by level, not by seat (see Shelf kinds below): aim anywhere on a board and click to put the hand's front item into the level's next free seat, or drop it on the board; click the level's stock to take the last item put in (see Moving items: one rule in `docs/CONTAINERS_AND_HAND.md`). `CardShelf` builds one `ShelfLevelTarget` per level, one box over the whole board and its stock, highlighted as one; it is on the interaction layer while the shelf is shown and the level holds anything or has room. `StockMarketValue` gives a card its `CardValue` (rarity x intensity tier, x `SpecialMultiplier` for a special card, never below `MinimumCardPrice`) and a booster or pack its `ProductMarketPrices` value. A card shelf has one `SlotPricing` price tag per seat and a product shelf one per level (`Scenes/Shop/Items/PriceTag.tscn`, a paper strip on the shelf's front face that prints the asking price of the stock it covers); a storage rack has none, since rack stock is never sold. A click opens the `PricingScreen` (`IPricingScreen`, a scene service under `Services`, an `IModalScreen` that holds the cursor and the player's controls) for that stock (`IPricedStock`): it names the item and what the price covers, shows the market price, the wholesale cost and profit per sale for bought-in stock, and the share of customers who buy at the entered price (`PricingSheet`, from `BuyDecision`), and only Set price changes anything; Cancel and Escape close it unchanged. A card's price is its own (`CardStockPrice`, stored on `CardController.ShopPrice`), so it travels with the card to any shelf. A booster or pack carries its own price too (`CardContainer.ShopPrice`), and every item on one product-shelf level asks the same price (`ShelfPlacementRule.PriceOnPut`: the first item on an empty level keeps its own and so sets it, every later item takes it, and a taken item keeps the price it had). The level's tag prices the whole level (`ContainerStockPrice`, never products elsewhere), shows the price of the item a customer takes next, and reads the wholesale cost from `IWholesaleCosts` (the `WholesaleCosts` node, over `BackofficeCatalog.tres`). A box is never sold and has no price. Prices stay within `ShopPrice.Min` and `ShopPrice.Max` (1 to 9999). A level only takes the bodies its shelf kind accepts (see Shelf kinds below), so a box on a card shelf is ignored. The shop scene has the card services and a `CardSpawner` (`World/Cards`, over open storefront floor away from the counter; keys 1, 2, 3 spawn random cards, key 4 spawns one special card) so cards can be spawned for testing.

**Shelf kinds.** `CardShelf` has a `Kind` (`ShelfKind`, in `Scripts/Features/Shop/Models/ShelfKind.cs`; the per-kind stock table is `ShelfPlacementRule.Accepts`): a card shelf (`Shelf.tscn`, the default, levels `Stands` and `Rests`), a product shelf (`ProductShelf.tscn`, four levels of four seats: `BottomBoard`, `LowerMiddleBoard`, `UpperMiddleBoard` and `TopBoard`; a seat holds one pack alone or a stack of up to `ShelfPlacementRule.BoostersPerProductSlot` (4) boosters, so the shelf holds 16 packs or 64 boosters) or a storage rack (`StorageRack.tscn`, levels `BottomRow` and `MiddleRow` of two seats each, which take a box, a pack or a booster; the rack's art and colliders are scaled by 1.06, `ArtScale`, so a 0.669 m cardboard box on the middle level clears the board above it, which `ShelfSeatTest` checks). Each board is a `ShelfLevel` (`Scripts/Features/Shop/Components`): the unit the player stocks, prices and takes from, and an `IItemStore`. It holds one kind of stock at a time (`ShelfPlacementRule.LevelTakes`) and fills its seats in order, so an item put or dropped anywhere on the board goes into the next free seat and a take removes the last one put in; when an item leaves from the middle (a card picked up by hand, a load clearing the shelf) the later stock closes up. Its `DeckSlot` seats only lock and pose bodies (`LocksOnEntry` off); a lone `DeckSlot` outside a level (a workshop holder) still locks free stock that lands in its trigger. A level moves cards, booster packs and booster boxes as records; a cardboard box is no record, so it goes on and off a rack level as a body (`ShelfLevel.TryLock`). Level stock is saved in order with its prices and locked back into its level before any physics step (`ShelfStockSaveTest`). `Kind` sets which containers each level accepts and which group the shelf joins: a card shelf and a product shelf join `sales_shelves`, and a rack joins `storage_racks` only. Customers buy only from `sales_shelves` (`ShelfOfferIndex`), so rack stock is never sold. The player stocks a shelf one item at a time from the hand's front item or from an open cardboard box in hand (see Moving items: one rule under Container Meshes and Opening in `docs/CONTAINERS_AND_HAND.md`); only the restocker unpacks a whole cardboard box at once (`CardShelf.TryUnpack`: it moves what free seats take and keeps the rest in the box). A booster box or booster pack is never unpacked at a shelf; it is opened in the hand. Every shelf has a `ShelfFootprint` area (`CardShelf.FootprintVolume`) over its colliders' bounds, on no layer and in `DeliveryMarker.FootprintGroup`, so a delivered shelf stacks as its bounding box even though it collides as separate boards; it is an area, not a body, because the navigation bake parses static bodies even on a carried shelf. Each price tag (`Scenes/Shop/Items/PriceTag.tscn`) is in the `DeliveryMarker.NoFootprintGroup` group, so its solid shape never counts toward a delivered shelf's footprint (the opposite of `DeliveryMarker.FootprintGroup`, which adds a trigger to it) and the next layer rests on the shelf box, not on its tags.
